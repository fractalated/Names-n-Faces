Manage Roster

Import from a PDF, add students one at a time, or manage what's already saved.

Manage Users  ·  ← Home

Upload a class roster PDF. Each page is scanned to find whatever grouping label is printed at the top (a period, grade level, or gender, for example) plus every student's name and photo. Nothing is saved until you review and confirm below — you can adjust the detected group name or pick an existing one before saving, and importing a group replaces everything currently saved for it.
